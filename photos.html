Photos & Memories

Moments from my postdoctoral research at Carnegie Mellon University, Pittsburgh, USA (February 2024 – July 2025), with colleagues and mentors.

Research at Carnegie Mellon University

During my postdoctoral stay in the Department of Engineering and Public Policy (EPP) at Carnegie Mellon University, I worked under the supervision of my advisor, Prof. Ramteen Sioshansi, and had the privilege of learning from Prof. M. Granger Morgan, Prof. Nicholas Muller, Prof. Jeremy Michalek, Prof. Costa Samaras and Prof. Peter Adams. Our one-on-one research discussions, attending their lectures and taking part in departmental seminars and workshops were truly valuable experiences. I learned a great deal from each of them, and this period shaped how I approach energy systems, electricity markets and energy policy. I am deeply grateful for their time, their generous feedback and the open, collaborative environment of EPP.
